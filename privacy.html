Privacy Policy

Grassberry Studio (Nori and Berry) runs a small private tool that publishes our own videos to our own social media pages, including our Facebook Page. Last updated 6 November 2026.

What we collect

The tool only accesses the pages and accounts we own. It does not collect, store or share personal information about anyone else, and it does not use Facebook Login for other people.

What we store

We keep a record of which of our videos were posted and their public view counts, to plan posting times.

Data deletion

We hold no data about other users. To ask about any data, or to request deletion, email grassberrystudio@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days.
